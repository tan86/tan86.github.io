---
title: Still for You
created: 2025-06-27
modified: 2026-11-25
tags: [poem, foryou]
description: A free verse on waiting for someone till the end of time, without ever saying it.
---

_A friend once shared a reel with me that said: write "I will wait for you till the end of time" without writing it. I wrote this and shared it with her._

I am an impatient man, my love,
I cannot bear the passing of time.
But if your last breath calls me,
I'd rise, even from my grave.

Against what's written,
I carve your name.
Even in dreams,
my blind soul seeks you.

I don't mind the stillness,
if it's you I'm still for.
I would lose myself
a thousand times
just to find you once.

It's torture.
nothing kills me like you.
you suffocate me deeper than this smoke,
burn me more than these embers,
yet keep me alive
with the cruel sweetness of hope.
